What's Inside

Key ingredients we stand by

Vitamin C

Brightens uneven skin tone and protects against UV-induced free radical damage — essential under Singapore's year-round sun.

Niacinamide

Regulates sebum, minimises pores, and reduces hyperpigmentation. Works especially well in humidity-prone environments.

Hyaluronic Acid

Draws moisture from the air into skin cells. Lightweight and non-occlusive — ideal for tropical climates.

Centella Asiatica

Reduces inflammation, speeds up healing, and reinforces the skin barrier after sun or heat exposure.

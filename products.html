What's Inside

Key ingredients we stand by

Vitamin C

A powerful antioxidant that brightens uneven skin tone and protects against UV-induced free radical damage — essential in Singapore's year-round sun.

Niacinamide

Regulates sebum production, minimises pores, and reduces hyperpigmentation. Works especially well in humidity-prone environments.

Hyaluronic Acid

Draws moisture from the air into skin cells. Lightweight and non-occlusive — ideal for layering in tropical climates without feeling heavy.

Centella Asiatica

A calming botanical that reduces inflammation, speeds up healing, and reinforces the skin barrier — popular in Korean skincare for good reason.

Build Your Routine

The Lueur Co. daily routine

01

Cleanse

Start with Soft Reset Cleanser to remove sweat and buildup from Singapore's humid air. AM & PM.

02

Treat

03

04

Protect

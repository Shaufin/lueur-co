Application

How to use

1

Cleanse first. Apply to freshly cleansed, slightly damp skin for better absorption.

2

Use 3–4 drops. Press gently into face and neck. Do not rub — pat gently.

3

Wait 60 seconds. Let the serum fully absorb before applying moisturiser.

4

Follow with SPF. Always apply SPF 50 in the morning after this serum.

5

Use AM & PM. Safe for twice-daily use. Store away from direct sunlight.

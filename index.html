Common Questions

Glow FAQs

Everything you need to know about caring for your skin in Singapore's heat — and how Lueur Co. fits into your daily routine.

Why does skin behave differently in hot, humid weather?

When temperature and humidity rise, skin tends to produce more oil while also losing moisture faster through sweat. This combination can leave skin feeling both greasy and dehydrated at the same time. Lueur Co. formulas are designed to balance this, giving skin a lightweight glow instead of a heavy or shiny finish, even on the hottest days.

Will the Glow Essence Serum feel heavy under makeup or sunscreen?

No. The serum is water-based and absorbs within seconds, leaving skin feeling smooth rather than sticky. This makes it easy to layer sunscreen on top without pilling, which is especially helpful during long days out in the heat.

How long until I see a visible glow?

Most customers report softer, brighter-looking skin within two to three weeks of consistent use, both morning and night. For best results, pair the serum with the Dew Veil Moisturiser and a daily SPF, as recommended by most dermatologists for tropical climates. For general guidance on building a skincare routine, resources like the American Academy of Dermatology offer helpful, science-based advice.

Is Lueur Co. suitable for sensitive or acne-prone skin?

Yes. All formulas are non-comedogenic, fragrance-free where possible, and tested specifically in heat and humidity to reduce the chance of breakouts and irritation. If you have a diagnosed skin condition, we always recommend checking with a dermatologist before introducing new active ingredients to your skin.

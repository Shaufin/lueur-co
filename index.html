About Lueur Co.

Born in Singapore.
Built for humidity.

Most skincare is formulated for temperate climates — dry winters and mild summers. Singapore is different. High heat, constant humidity, and UV exposure year-round demand a different kind of formula.

Lueur Co. was built from the ground up with Singapore's climate in mind. Every ingredient, texture, and formulation is tested here, for skin like yours.
